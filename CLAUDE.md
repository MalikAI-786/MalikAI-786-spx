## SPX publication routing

For any SPX morning email, close report, performance summary, dashboard refresh,
or portfolio commentary, read **[scripts/SKILL-accountability.md](scripts/SKILL-accountability.md)** after the task-specific skill.

The accountability layer is mandatory. It requires the next publication to lead
with the prior published setup's verified synthetic result, cumulative verified
P&L, evidence coverage, and a direction-vs-structure-vs-discipline diagnosis.
It also keeps real portfolio activity private unless the owner explicitly sets a
disclosure level.
